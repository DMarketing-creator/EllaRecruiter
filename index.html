6 Stage 06

Ella AI: Send Interview Invitation

Once approved by HR, Ella emails shortlisted candidates a calendar link so they can book their phone interview.

7 Stage 07

Ella AI: Conduct Phone Interview

Ella asks up to 5 screening questions: up to 2 customized by the HOD, with the remaining questions generated by Ella. If no HOD questions are provided, Ella generates all 5. Results are sent to HR.

8 Stage 08

HR Next Interview Decision: Approve / Reject

HR reviews the Ella phone interview result and decides whether the candidate will proceed to the next interview with the HOD.

9 Stage 09

Ella AI: Send Next Interview Invitation

Once approved by HR, Ella schedules the next interview, face-to-face with the HOD, using the HOD's available dates and times already pre-set in the Requisition Form, and sends the invitation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here's everything you need to know about getting started with Ella AI.

Is there a free trial available? expand_more

Yes. You can try Ella with 15 one-time trial credits, valid for 30 days. No subscription or credit card is required to begin the trial.

Can I upgrade to Ella Credits later? expand_more

Yes. You can start with the free trial and purchase Ella Credits anytime when you are ready to scale hiring activity.

How do Ella Credits work? expand_more

AI CV Analysis uses 1 credit per resume, and AI Phone Interview uses 10 credits per candidate. Credits are consumed only when actions are used, purchased credits stay valid for 1 year, and buying 2,000+ credits in one transaction gets you 10% off automatically.

Does the AI make the final hiring decision? expand_more

No. AI CV Analysis and AI Phone Interview are designed to assist your HR team, not replace it. Every recommendation is reviewed by a person before a candidate moves forward, and the final HR Interview stage is scheduled and conducted by your team.

Is my data safe and secure? expand_more

Yes. We apply secure workflow practices and can discuss enterprise-grade support and custom requirements for larger teams.

How can I get support if I encounter an issue? expand_more

Free Trial and Ella Credits plans include email support. Enterprise plans may include a dedicated account manager and custom support arrangements.
